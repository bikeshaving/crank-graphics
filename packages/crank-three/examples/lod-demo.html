Level of Detail (LOD) Demonstration

The sphere automatically switches between different detail levels based on camera distance:

High Detail (0-8 units): 1024 triangles
Medium Detail (8-15 units): 256 triangles
Low Detail (15-25 units): 64 triangles
Billboard (>25 units): 2 triangles

Performance Benefit: Distant objects use fewer triangles, improving frame rate while maintaining visual quality up close.
